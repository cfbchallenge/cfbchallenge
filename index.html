The proposal in 30 seconds

  • Big-time nonconference games: conference-wide Challenge weekends replace low-stakes scheduling.
  • Conference divisions: smaller races restore rivalries and reward winning.
  • Playoff berths on the line: 17 of 24 places are earned through on-field qualification.
  • Transparent rankings: published, results-based criteria determine seeding.
  • A regular season that matters: Challenge outcomes, division titles and seeding carry real consequences.
